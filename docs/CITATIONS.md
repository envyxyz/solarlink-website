## 5. What to skip

- Any directory that charges to appear in its results, unless the owner has judged it a real lead source.
- Mass submission tools and "500 listings for a fee" services.
- Listings in cities with no staffed office.
- Any site that asks for a block different from the approved one. Do not adapt the block to fit a form; write to the site instead.
