### Could Not Verify
1. **Feeder-by-Feeder Real-Time Outage Database:** While aggregate annual SAIFI and feeder category loss brackets are published by NEPRA and the Power Division, real-time hourly telemetry per 11kV feeder is not publicly aggregated in an accessible open API or database by DISCOs.
2. **Standardized Sub-Division Solar Demand Note Fees:** Exact CCE / Demand Note figures for bi-directional meters vary from Rs. 65,000 to Rs. 110,000 across individual sub-divisions depending on local meter procurement stock, meter testing laboratory fees, and CT/PT requirements for loads above 20 kW.
