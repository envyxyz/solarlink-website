## 2. Five Things These Sites Do Well

1. **Clear Audience Segmentation & Targeted Product Lines:** Leading players like Reon Energy and SkyElectric segment their websites cleanly by client scale (Residential SkyHome, SME SkyBiz, Industrial SkyIndus/Telecom). Reon focuses strictly on enterprise C&I and microgrids, eliminating consumer clutter to appeal directly to industrial decision-makers.
2. **Context-Rich Lead Qualification on Inbound Forms:** Premier Energy's primary inquiry form asks for "Monthly Electric Units" and "Monthly Electric Bill" alongside standard contact fields. This enables sales engineers to instantly calculate system requirements, estimate net-metering eligibility, and prioritize leads before initiating contact.
3. **Robust Social Proof & Verifiable Local Authority:** Both SkyElectric and Premier Energy heavily feature verifiable local credibility markers: cumulative nationwide figures (e.g., SkyElectric's "10,000+ Customers" and "250+ MW"), embedded YouTube customer testimonial videos, and project photos tagged with specific Pakistani cities.
4. **Appliance-Level Solar Load Estimation:** Premier Energy's interactive Solar Calculator (`/solar-calculator/`) allows users to tally everyday household appliances (lights, fans, inverter ACs, deep freezers) to understand their total connected load and see what capacity system they actually need.
5. **High-Tech Positioning Beyond Commodity Panels:** SkyElectric and Reon Energy frame themselves as proprietary energy technology companies rather than generic equipment installers. They emphasize custom energy management software (SPARK™ EMS, SkyElectric Smart Inverter App) and 24/7 cloud network operations monitoring (NOC).

## 3. Five Things None of Them Does

1. **Transparent Upfront Pricing & Ballpark Cost Tiers:** None of the evaluated websites display clear, upfront turnkey prices, estimated cost brackets per kilowatt (e.g., benchmark PKR cost for a standard 5kW, 10kW, or 15kW on-grid system), or component price breakdowns. All force visitors through a closed sales funnel.
2. **Instant Dynamic ROI & Payback Calculators:** While Premier Energy offers an appliance load calculator, none of the websites provide an instant, interactive financial simulation showing estimated monthly electricity bill savings, net-metering export credits, and estimated payback periods (in years/months) tied to current DISCO/NEPRA slab tariffs.
3. **Interactive 3D Roof Visualizers or Digital Solar Mapping:** None of the sites utilize modern 3D WebGL (Three.js/Canvas) tools, satellite roof-mapping integrations, or interactive panel configuration tools that allow prospective buyers to visualize an installation on their roof type.
4. **Urdu Language Support & Regional Accessibility:** Despite serving a nationwide customer base—including domestic homeowners, small business owners, and agricultural tubewell operators across Punjab, Sindh, and KPK—all Pakistani sites operate exclusively in English. None offer an Urdu interface, bilingual toggle, or localized audio-visual guides.
5. **Real-Time Inventory Status & Transparent Component Comparison:** None of the sites feature an interactive hardware comparison tool or real-time equipment availability dashboard (e.g., comparing N-Type TOPCon vs. P-Type Monocrystalline efficiency, inverter warranty terms, or battery cycle life) with transparent, downloadable datasheets in a clean side-by-side comparison format.
