# Pakistan Rooftop Solar Net Billing Verification Summary

## Primary Legal Framework
- **Primary Instrument**: NEPRA (Prosumer) Regulations, 2026, notified via S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 (2026-02-09).
- **Amendments**: S.R.O. 547(I)/2026 (2026-04-02, grandfathering) & S.R.O. 1330(I)/2026 (2026-08-06, <=25 kW exemption).
- **Repealed Instrument**: S.R.O. 812(I)/2015 (ARE Distributed Generation and Net Metering Regulations, 2015).

## Verification Findings & Figure Verdicts
1. **Export Buyback Rate [DIFFERENT]**: S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 Reg 14(1)(b) links buyback to the National Average Energy Purchase Price (NAEPP), not a static Rs 10.00-11.30/kWh or Rs 11.0 benchmark. Under NEPRA CY2026 Determination TRF-100/CPPAG (2026-01-07), NAEPP is Rs 8.13/kWh (NAPPP is Rs 25.32/kWh).
2. **Net Billing Mechanism [CONFIRMED]**: Reg 14(1) terminates 1:1 volumetric netting for new prosumers. Imports are billed at applicable consumer retail tariffs; surplus exports are credited separately at NAEPP.
3. **Settlement Period [CONFIRMED]**: Reg 2(1)(viii) defines a 30-day billing cycle. Under Reg 14(2), excess monetary credits rollover to offset subsequent monthly bills or are paid out quarterly by the licensee.
4. **Sanctioned Load Limit [CONFIRMED]**: Reg 3(2) restricts distributed generation capacity to <= 100% of consumer sanctioned load (revoking the 2015 allowance permitting up to 150%).
5. **Transformer Hosting Limit [CONFIRMED]**: Reg 3(5) second proviso strictly bars DISCOs from entertaining applications once generation reaches 80% of rated distribution transformer capacity.
6. **Concurrence Exemption <= 25 kW [CONFIRMED]**: S.R.O. 1330(I)/2026 (Reg 4(1) second proviso) waives separate NEPRA concurrence for systems <= 25 kW; approval is accorded directly by the DISCO.
7. **Capacity Upper Cap [CONFIRMED]**: Reg 2(1)(ix) & Schedule-I establish a maximum generation capacity limit of 1 MW (1,000 kW) for solar, wind, and biogas facilities.
8. **Load Flow Feasibility [CONFIRMED]**: Reg 3(3) mandates an interconnection load flow study for facilities >= 250 kW conducted via the DISCO or a PEC-registered consultant.
9. **NEPRA Concurrence Fee [CONFIRMED]**: Schedule-IV specifies a one-time fee of Rs 1,000/kW for concurrence applications (>25 kW). Systems <= 25 kW pay Rs 0 due to statutory concurrence exemption.
10. **DISCO / LESCO Fees [DIFFERENT]**: Primary regulations do not prescribe fixed fee brackets (e.g. Rs 15k-25k processing or Rs 35k-75k meter). Reg 3(9) & Reg 12(1) mandate individualized Connection Charge Estimates / Demand Notices based on actual hardware costs. EPC liaison fees are unregulated market rates.
11. **Grandfathering Term [DIFFERENT]**: S.R.O. 547(I)/2026 protects legacy 2015 agreements "till the expiry of the term of such agreement", without citing a "7-year term". The statutory contract term under S.R.O. 812(I)/2015 Reg 7(1) was 3 years (renewable), while S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 Reg 7(1) sets 5 years. Forfeiture of grandfathering upon capacity expansion or material modification is confirmed.
