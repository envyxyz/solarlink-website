# Pakistan Rooftop Solar Net Metering / Net Billing Regulations (As of November 2026)

## 1. Regulatory Regime & Policy Shift
- **Current Legal Framework**: In effect under the **NEPRA (Prosumer) Regulations, 2026**, notified via **S.R.O. 251(I)/2026** on **February 9, 2026**.
  - *Source (Official)*: NEPRA Notification S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 (2026-02-09), [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk).
- **Repeal of 2015 Rules**: Formally repealed and replaced the *NEPRA (Alternative & Renewable Energy) Distributed Generation and Net Metering Regulations, 2015* (S.R.O. 812(I)/2015, 2015-09-01).
- **Subsequent Amendments**:
  - **S.R.O. 547(I)/2026 (2026-04-02)**: Retrospective amendment to Regulation 21 establishing explicit grandfathering protections.
  - **S.R.O. 1330(I)/2026 (2026-08-06)**: Streamlined approval; exempted systems $\le$ 25 kW from direct NEPRA concurrence.
  - *Source (Official)*: NEPRA Notifications S.R.O. 547(I)/2026 and S.R.O. 1330(I)/2026, [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk).
- **Gross Metering vs Net Billing**: The Power Division's mid-2024 proposal for "gross metering" (mandatory sale of 100% solar generation at wholesale rates while buying all consumption at retail) was shelved after public pushback. Instead, **net billing** was enacted: prosumers retain self-consumption behind the meter and export only surplus.
  - *Source (Secondary - News)*: Business Recorder / Dawn Policy Reports (2024-05-15), [brecorder.com](https://brecorder.com).

## 2. Billing Structure & Settlement Mechanism
- **Separate Billing (Not Netted per kWh)**: Unlike the 1:1 volumetric netting under the 2015 regulations, energy is accounted for separately:
  - **Grid Imports**: Charged at standard retail consumer-end slab tariffs (including FCAs, quarterly adjustments, and taxes).
  - **Grid Exports**: Surplus energy injected is credited separately at the designated buyback rate.
  - **Net Calculation**: Net Bill = $(\text{Imported kWh} \times \text{Retail Tariff}) - (\text{Exported kWh} \times \text{Buyback Rate})$.
  - *Source (Official)*: S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 Regulation 11 & 14 (2026-02-09), [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk).
- **Settlement Period**: Standard **monthly** billing cycle. If the monetary export credit exceeds the monthly consumption bill, the remaining monetary credit rolls over to offset future electricity bills.
  - *Source (Official)*: S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 (2026-02-09), [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk).

## 3. Export (Buy-Back) Rate & Determination
- **Buy-Back Rate**: Set at **Rs. 10.00 – 11.30 per kWh** (benchmark ~Rs. 11.00/kWh, reduced from legacy levels of Rs. 22–27/kWh).
- **Determination Method**: Calculated based on the **National Average Energy Purchase Price (NAEPP / EPP)**—the avoided variable fuel and generation cost determined by NEPRA, strictly excluding capacity payments, transmission charges, and distribution margins.
  - *Source (Official)*: NEPRA Power Purchase Price Determinations & S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 (2026-02-09), [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk).

## 4. System Capacity & Technical Limits
- **Sanctioned Load Cap**: Distributed generation facility capacity **cannot exceed 100% of the consumer's sanctioned load** (the 2015 allowance permitting up to 150% was revoked). Load enhancement is required prior to applying for larger systems.
- **Maximum System Size**: Up to **1 MW (1,000 kW)** for distributed generation under these regulations.
- **Distribution Transformer Limit**: Cumulative distributed generation on any distribution transformer is capped at **80% of its rated capacity**.
- **Load Flow Study**: Mandatory for systems $\ge$ **250 kW** (conducted by a PEC-registered consultant or DISCO).
- **Concurrence Exemption**: Systems $\le$ **25 kW** require only DISCO approval; direct NEPRA concurrence is waived.
  - *Source (Official)*: S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 & S.R.O. 1330(I)/2026 (2026-08-06), [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk).

## 5. Application Steps & Fees (LESCO / IESCO / FESCO)
- **Application Process**:
  1. *Site Design & Validation*: AEDB/PPIB-certified installer and PEC engineer design system and confirm 3-phase connection & sanctioned load.
  2. *Dossier Submission*: Submit application form, Single Line Diagram (SLD), equipment spec sheets (IEC 62109 compliant inverters, Tier-1 panels), CNIC, paid bill, and indemnity bond to local DISCO Sub-Division Office (SDO Operation) or online portal.
  3. *Technical Scrutiny*: DISCO assesses transformer capacity (80% limit) and conducts load flow feasibility.
  4. *Demand Notice & Payment*: DISCO issues Demand Notice / Connection Estimate covering bi-directional meter and testing fees.
  5. *Inspection & Testing*: DISCO engineers inspect installation, verify anti-islanding, earthing resistance, and safety isolators.
  6. *Agreement & Commissioning*: Prosumer signs Distributed Generation Agreement (Schedule-I); DISCO Meter Testing (MT) lab installs bi-directional (green) meter and energizes the connection.
- **Fee Schedule**:
  - **NEPRA Concurrence Fee**: **Nil** for systems $\le$ 25 kW; **PKR 1,000 per kW** for systems > 25 kW.
    - *Source (Official)*: S.R.O. 1330(I)/2026 & NEPRA Fee Schedule, [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk).
  - **DISCO Processing / Inspection Fee**: **PKR 15,000 – 25,000** (official DISCO demand notice).
  - **Bi-directional (Green) Meter & Testing**: **PKR 35,000 – 75,000+** depending on 3-phase meter model and DISCO testing lab rates.
    - *Source (Official/DISCO)*: LESCO / IESCO / FESCO Net Metering Consumer Guidelines, [lesco.gov.pk](https://lesco.gov.pk).
  - **Turnkey EPC Liaison Fee**: **PKR 70,000 – 110,000** (market-based EPC liaison and engineering documentation fee).
    - *Source (Secondary - Industry/Market)*: Solar Citizen / Pak Solar Insights Guides, [solarcitizen.com.pk](https://solarcitizen.com.pk).

## 6. Grandfathering of Existing Users
- **Protected Category**: Consumers who signed valid distributed generation agreements under the repealed 2015 regulations prior to February 9, 2026.
- **Terms Protected**: Maintain their original **1:1 volumetric net metering** billing mechanism and legacy export settlement rates.
- **Duration**: Valid for the remaining term of their existing agreement (standard contract tenure of **7 years** from agreement execution date).
- **Forfeiture Clause**: If an existing user makes any "material modification" or expands their inverter/generation capacity, grandfathering is forfeited and the entire installation converts to the 2026 net billing regime.
  - *Source (Official)*: NEPRA S.R.O. 547(I)/2026 amending Regulation 21 (2026-04-02), [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk).
