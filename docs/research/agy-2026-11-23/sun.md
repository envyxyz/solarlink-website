## 4. Seasonal Dynamics & Engineering Implications
- **Peak Generation Window:** April–May delivers maximum irradiation (6.3–6.7 PSH) before extreme summer temperatures induce PV thermal derating.
- **Monsoon Impact:** July–August shows noticeable dip (down to 4.76–5.58 PSH) across Punjab due to extensive cloud cover and precipitation.
- **Winter Smog / Fog:** Lahore suffers pronounced December–January dip (3.54–3.84 PSH) exacerbated by seasonal atmospheric aerosol accumulation.
