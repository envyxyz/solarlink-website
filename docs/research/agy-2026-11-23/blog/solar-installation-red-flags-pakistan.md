## 3. Key Sourced Facts (8–15)
1. **Unregistered AEDB/PPIB Status**: AEDB/PPIB maintains categorized licensing (C-3 to C-A); unregistered contractors cannot legally submit or approve net metering dossiers with DISCOs. (*PPIB Solar Installer Registration Regulations*, 2024; [ppib.gov.pk](https://ppib.gov.pk)).
2. **Missing PEC Engineer Seal**: Under Pakistan Engineering Council bye-laws, solar Single Line Diagrams (SLD) and structural designs must be validated and stamped by a licensed Professional Engineer (PE). (*PEC Regulations / LESCO Net Metering SOP*, 2024; [lesco.org.pk](https://lesco.org.pk)).
3. **B-Grade / Counterfeit Panels**: Fraudulent vendors sell flash-test failed B/C-grade panels with counterfeit Tier-1 stickers; genuine panels feature embedded barcodes under the tempered glass matching factory flash test reports. (*Clean Energy Council PV Quality Guide / PCRET Advisory*, 2024; [pcret.gov.pk](https://pcret.gov.pk)).
4. **Improper DC Cabling**: Using non-solar domestic AC wiring or copper-clad aluminum (CCA) instead of certified UV-resistant, flame-retardant tinned copper solar cables (EN 50618 / IEC 62930) causes severe insulation degradation and rooftop fires. (*IEC 62930:2017 Standard / NFPA 70 National Electrical Code*).
5. **Substandard Mounting Structures**: Fabricating frames from thin non-galvanized mild steel painted with red oxide instead of hot-dip galvanized steel (minimum 80–100 micron zinc coating per ASTM A123) leads to rust and structural failure during 100+ km/h dust storms. (*ASTM A123/A123M Standards / PEC Wind Load Code*).
6. **Inadequate Earthing & Missing SPDs**: Omitting DC/AC Type-II Surge Protection Devices (SPDs) or failing to achieve an earth pit resistance below 5 Ohms voids inverter warranties and creates lethal shock hazards. (*IEC 62305 Lightning Protection Standard / NEPRA DG Guidelines*, 2024; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
7. **100% Upfront Payment Demands**: Reputable EPC firms operate on milestone-based payment schedules (e.g., 20% advance, 60% on equipment site delivery, 15% commissioning, 5% post-metering retention); 100% upfront cash demands represent a major scam risk. (*Punjab Consumer Protection Council Advisory*, 2023).
8. **Physically Impossible Generation Claims**: Claiming a 5kW system can generate 30+ units daily year-round defies solar physics (annual average in Punjab is ~4.0–4.2 units/day per kWp). (*Global Solar Atlas 2.0 / World Bank ESMAP*, 2026-11-23; [globalsolaratlas.info](https://globalsolaratlas.info)).
9. **No Physical Office or Warehouse**: Shell vendors operating exclusively through social media pages without registered commercial premises or stock warehouses frequently dissolve when warranty claims arise. (*SECP Corporate Guidelines*, 2023; [secp.gov.pk](https://secp.gov.pk)).
10. **Refusal to Provide Written Net-Metering SLA**: Unqualified installers complete mechanical mounting but abandon clients when DISCO SDO technical objections or transformer saturation hurdles occur. (*AEDB Model EPC Consumer Agreement*, 2023; [ppib.gov.pk](https://ppib.gov.pk)).
