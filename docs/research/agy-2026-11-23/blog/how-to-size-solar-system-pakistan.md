## 3. Key Sourced Facts (8–15)
1. **Solar Resource Yield**: Lahore averages 5.08 Peak Sun Hours (PSH) daily (1,854.7 kWh/m²/yr GTI at 28° tilt); Islamabad averages 5.38 PSH daily (1,964.6 kWh/m²/yr GTI at 30° tilt). (*Global Solar Atlas 2.0 / World Bank ESMAP*, 2026-11-23; [globalsolaratlas.info](https://globalsolaratlas.info)).
2. **Specific Annual Generation**: 1 kWp of installed PV generates ~1,465 kWh/year in Lahore and ~1,559 kWh/year in Islamabad, translating to ~3.8–4.3 kWh/kWp/day averaged over the year. (*PVGIS-ERA5 / European Commission JRC*, 2024; [re.jrc.ec.europa.eu](https://re.jrc.ec.europa.eu/pvg_tools/en/)).
3. **Seasonal Generation Swing**: Lahore solar irradiance drops to 3.54 PSH in foggy January and peaks at 6.37 PSH in April; sizing must account for winter smog dips and summer AC peaks. (*Global Solar Atlas 2.0*, 2026-11-23).
4. **Engineering Sizing Formula**: Required Array Size (kWp) = (Annual kWh Consumption / 365) / (Daily PSH × System PR of 0.75–0.80). (*IEC 61724-1 Standard*, 2021).
5. **Sanctioned Load Legal Cap**: NEPRA Prosumer Regulations stipulate installed distributed generation capacity cannot exceed 100% of the consumer's sanctioned load on their utility bill. (*NEPRA S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 Reg 4*, 2026-02-09; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
6. **3-Phase Interconnection Requirement**: DISCOs (LESCO, IESCO) require a 3-phase meter for solar connections exceeding 5 kW and all net-metered sites. (*LESCO Net Metering SOP*, 2024; [lesco.org.pk](https://lesco.org.pk)).
7. **Transformer Saturation Limit**: Cumulative solar capacity on any local 11kV distribution transformer is legally capped at 80% of transformer rated kVA. (*NEPRA S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 Reg 6*, 2026-02-09; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
8. **Rooftop Area Footprint**: High-efficiency 580W–590W N-type panels occupy ~28 sq.ft (2.6 m²) each; a 10 kW system (17–18 panels) requires 550–650 sq.ft of unshaded roof. (*Longi Hi-MO X6 Datasheet*, 2024; [longi.com](https://www.longi.com)).
9. **Seasonal Consumption Ratio**: Urban Pakistani domestic consumption in peak summer (May–Aug) is 2.5x to 3.5x higher than winter base load due to inverter air conditioning. (*AEDB Energy Profile Advisory*, 2023; [ppib.gov.pk](https://ppib.gov.pk)).
10. **Tariff Slab Optimization**: Sizing to reduce monthly grid import below 300 or 200 units avoids penal un-subsidized retail tariffs (>Rs. 39–48/kWh base). (*NEPRA Uniform Tariff S.R.O. 1035(I)/2024*, 2024-07-12; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
