## 3. Key Sourced Facts (8–15)
1. **On-Grid Anti-Islanding Protection**: On-grid string inverters must shut down automatically within <2 seconds upon loss of grid voltage to comply with IEC 62116 / IEEE 1547 and NEPRA Grid Code. (*NEPRA Grid Code / IEC 62116 Standards*, 2014; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
2. **Hybrid Inverter Transfer Speed**: Quality hybrid inverters (e.g. Deye, Growatt, GoodWe) feature an integrated automatic transfer switch (ATS) with <10–20 millisecond transfer time, preventing computer restarts during outages. (*Manufacturer Technical Specifications*, 2024).
3. **Off-Grid Autonomy Requirements**: Off-grid standalone systems require solar arrays and battery banks sized for 1.5 to 2.0 days of autonomy to survive prolonged winter fog or monsoon cloud cover. (*NREL Stand-Alone PV Systems Guide*, 2022; [nrel.gov](https://www.nrel.gov)).
4. **Conversion Efficiency Comparison**: On-grid string inverters operate at 97.5%–98.6% efficiency; hybrid inverters achieve 95%–97% in grid mode, but battery round-trip drops overall cycle efficiency to 85%–90%. (*SMA / Huawei / Sungrow Datasheets*, 2024).
5. **Net Metering Interconnection**: On-grid and hybrid systems holding AEDB/PPIB certification are fully eligible for net metering under S.R.O. 251(I)/2026; pure off-grid standalone inverters are prohibited from grid interconnection. (*NEPRA S.R.O. 251(I)/2026*, 2026-02-09; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
6. **System Capital Cost Comparison**: On-grid systems are the most economical (approx. PKR 110–135/W); hybrid systems cost 30%–50% more due to hybrid inverters and batteries; off-grid systems carry high storage costs. (*Renewable First Market Survey*, 2024-10-15 [Secondary - Industry Report]).
7. **Feeder-Specific Geographic Fit**: On-grid is optimal for Zero-Load-Shedding urban feeders (e.g., DHA/Bahria Lahore/Islamabad); hybrid is critical for feeders experiencing 2–6 hours daily load shedding; off-grid is required for remote farms and tube wells without WAPDA infrastructure. (*AEDB Consumer Guidance*, 2024; [ppib.gov.pk](https://ppib.gov.pk)).
8. **Recurring Lifecycle Costs**: On-grid systems require zero battery replacement over 25 years; hybrid/off-grid systems incur battery replacement expenses every 3–4 years (tubular) or 10–12 years (LiFePO4). (*PCRET Energy Storage Assessment*, 2023; [pcret.gov.pk](https://pcret.gov.pk)).
9. **Generator Integration**: Off-grid and advanced hybrid systems include dry-contact ATS triggers to auto-start backup diesel/petrol generators during consecutive sunless days. (*Inverter Engineering Manuals*, 2024).
