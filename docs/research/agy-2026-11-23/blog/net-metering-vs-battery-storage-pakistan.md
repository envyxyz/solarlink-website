## 3. Key Sourced Facts (8–15)
1. **Net Billing Tariff Spread**: Under NEPRA S.R.O. 251(I)/2026, exported solar power earns wholesale buyback (~Rs. 10.00–11.30/kWh), whereas imported grid power costs Rs. 30–48+/kWh (effective Rs. 55–68/kWh with taxes/FCAs), creating a ~Rs. 25–45/kWh spread that heavily penalizes grid reliance. (*NEPRA S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 & S.R.O. 1035(I)/2024*; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
2. **LiFePO4 Chemistry Longevity**: Modern Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) solar batteries achieve 4,000–6,000 cycles at 80%–90% Depth of Discharge (DoD) with a 10–12 year service life. (*Pylontech / Dyness Engineering Datasheets*, 2024).
3. **Tubular Lead-Acid Constraints**: Deep-cycle tubular batteries provide only 800–1,200 cycles at 50% DoD, suffer from 70%–75% round-trip efficiency, and require replacement every 2.5–3.5 years. (*PCRET Energy Storage Evaluation*, 2023; [pcret.gov.pk](https://pcret.gov.pk)).
4. **Levelized Cost of Storage (LCOS)**: LiFePO4 batteries in Pakistan yield an LCOS of approx. Rs. 18–25/kWh stored, which is cheaper than peak grid import tariffs (Rs. 45–65/kWh) but higher than export buyback credit (Rs. 11/kWh). (*IRENA Energy Storage Cost Benchmark 2023*; *LUMS Energy Institute*, 2024).
5. **Anti-Islanding Requirement**: Standard on-grid net-metered inverters shut down within <2 seconds during grid load shedding to comply with IEC 62116 / IEEE 1547 safety standards; batteries are mandatory for power continuity. (*IEC 62116 Standards*, 2014; [iec.ch](https://www.iec.ch)).
6. **Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) Comparison**: Adding a 10 kWh LiFePO4 battery pack adds approx. PKR 450,000 to PKR 650,000 to upfront system cost, compared to PKR 100,000 to PKR 150,000 for net metering meter and liaison fees. (*Market Survey*, 2024-10-15 [Secondary - Industry Survey]).
7. **Comparative Payback**: On-grid net metering alone achieves an equity payback period of 2.2 to 3.2 years for high-slab users; adding full battery backup extends payback to 4.5 to 6.0 years while providing 100% outage insurance. (*Renewable First Financial Model*, 2024 [Secondary - Industry Report]).
8. **Round-Trip Efficiency Loss**: Battery storage incurs a 5%–10% round-trip conversion loss in LiFePO4 and 25%–30% in tubular lead-acid, whereas net-metered virtual storage suffers zero chemical degradation loss. (*NREL PV & Battery Storage Analysis*, 2023; [nrel.gov](https://www.nrel.gov)).
9. **ToU Peak Shaving Value**: Discharging battery storage during DISCO evening peak hours (5 PM – 11 PM, billed at >Rs. 46–48/kWh base) eliminates the most expensive portion of a prosumer's bill. (*NEPRA ToU Billing Schedule*, 2024; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
