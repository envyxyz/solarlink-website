## 3. Key Sourced Facts (8–15)
1. **LESCO Service Jurisdiction**: LESCO supplies electricity to over 6 million consumers across Lahore, Kasur, Sheikhupura, and Okara districts. (*LESCO Annual Operational Report*, 2024; [lesco.org.pk](https://lesco.org.pk)).
2. **Current Regulatory Basis**: Interconnection and net billing are governed by NEPRA (Prosumer) Regulations, 2026, notified via S.R.O. 251(I)/2026. (*NEPRA S.R.O. 251(I)/2026*, 2026-02-09; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
3. **Application Intake Channel**: Applications are lodged on the PITC Distributed Generation Portal and physically submitted to the local SDO Operation sub-division. (*PITC DG Portal*, 2025–2026; [pitc.com.pk](https://pitc.com.pk)).
4. **Mandatory Documentation Dossier**: Requires Application Form-I, CNIC copy, latest paid electricity bill, Single Line Diagram (SLD) signed by a PEC engineer, equipment datasheets (IEC 62109/61727), AEDB installer certificate, and undertaking on judicial stamp paper. (*LESCO Net Metering SOP*, 2024; [lesco.org.pk](https://lesco.org.pk)).
5. **Technical Feasibility Timeline**: SDO Operation evaluates 3-phase supply, sanctioned load, and 11kV transformer saturation (80% ceiling) within 15 working days. (*LESCO Citizen Charter*, 2024; [lesco.org.pk](https://lesco.org.pk)).
6. **Concurrence Exemption for ≤25 kW**: Systems ≤25 kW are approved directly by LESCO without NEPRA concurrence, saving 15–20 days of inter-agency processing. (*NEPRA S.R.O. 1330(I)/2026*, 2026-08-06; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
7. **Bi-directional Meter Charges**: LESCO issues a Demand Note for green meter procurement and testing fees, typically ranging from PKR 35,000 to PKR 75,000 depending on meter capacity and stock. (*LESCO Schedule of Service Charges*, 2024; [lesco.org.pk](https://lesco.org.pk)).
8. **EPC Liaison Fees**: Market turnkey liaison fees charged by AEDB-registered installers range from PKR 70,000 to PKR 110,000 for end-to-end processing and stamping. (*Market Survey*, 2024-10-20 [Secondary - EPC Survey]).
9. **M&T Inspection Standards**: LESCO Metering & Testing (M&T) division tests anti-islanding disconnect (<2 seconds) and verifies earth resistance is strictly under 5 Ohms. (*LESCO M&T Operational Manual*, 2024).
10. **Agreement Term**: Schedule-I Distributed Generation Agreement is executed for 5 years under the 2026 regulations. (*NEPRA S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 Schedule-I*, 2026-02-09; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
11. **Real-World Timeline**: Official turnaround is 30–45 days; real-world Lahore installation-to-metering turnaround averages 6 to 10 weeks, largely dictated by green meter inventory at LESCO central stores. (*SolarLink Operations Audit*, 2026 [Installer Data]).
