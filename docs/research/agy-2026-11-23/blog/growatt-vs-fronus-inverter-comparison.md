## 3. Key Sourced Facts (8–15)
1. **Growatt Global Standing**: S&P Global ranked Growatt among the top 4 global PV inverter suppliers for distributed generation in 2023. (*Growatt Corporate Report*, 2024; [ginverter.com](https://www.ginverter.com)).
2. **Growatt On-Grid Efficiency**: Growatt MID 17-25KTL3-X on-grid models reach 98.75% max efficiency (98.5% European efficiency) with IP66 ingress protection. (*Growatt Datasheet*, 2024; [ginverter.com/datasheets](https://www.ginverter.com)).
3. **Fronus Brand Origins**: Fronus is a Pakistani private label importing OEM inverters (predominantly Voltronic/Axpert topologies) customized for local load-shedding. (*Fronus Pakistan Product Catalog*, 2024; [fronus.net.pk](http://fronus.net.pk)).
4. **Net Metering Standards**: NEPRA mandates all grid-tied inverters comply with IEC 62109-1/2 (safety) and IEC 61727 / IEEE 1547 (utility interface/anti-islanding). (*NEPRA S.R.O. 251(I)/2026*, 2026-02-09; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
5. **AEDB Certification**: Growatt on-grid string series (MIN, MOD, MID, MAC) are officially certified on AEDB/PPIB approved equipment lists for net metering; many budget Fronus hybrid models lack on-grid IEC certification and operate solely in off-grid/UPS mode. (*PPIB Approved Inverter List*, 2024; [ppib.gov.pk](https://ppib.gov.pk)).
6. **Dual AC Output Feature**: Fronus hybrid models (e.g., Platinum, Infini series) feature dual AC outputs allowing intelligent shedding of heavy loads during grid outages. (*Fronus Technical Specifications*, 2024; [fronus.net.pk](http://fronus.net.pk)).
7. **Warranty Terms**: Growatt on-grid provides a 5-year standard international factory warranty extendable to 10 years, whereas Fronus provides a 1-to-2-year local distributor repair warranty. (*Growatt Warranty Policy 2024*; *Fronus Warranty Card 2024*).
8. **Thermal Derating & Ingress**: Growatt on-grid units are IP65/IP66 rated (outdoor safe) and derate above 45°C; budget Fronus units are IP21 (indoor only) and require dust-filtered environments in Punjab summers. (*Manufacturer Technical Manuals*, 2023–2024).
9. **Market Price Differential**: Fronus hybrid inverters typically retail 15%–25% lower than comparable capacity Growatt hybrid units in Pakistani wholesale markets. (*SolarMarket.pk Price Survey*, 2024-10-15 [Secondary - Marketplace]; [solarmarket.pk](https://solarmarket.pk)).
10. **Battery Protocol Support**: Growatt SPH/SPF supports native CAN/RS485 BMS communication for major LiFePO4 batteries; entry Fronus models rely on voltage-based lead-acid/tubular charging curves. (*Growatt & Fronus User Manuals*, 2024).
