## 3. Key Sourced Facts (8–15)
1. **Sanctioned Load Discrepancy**: NEPRA regulations prohibit DG facilities larger than the consumer's sanctioned load; mismatch triggers immediate DISCO dossier rejection. (*NEPRA S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 Reg 4*, 2026-02-09; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
2. **80% Transformer Limit**: DISCOs cannot approve net metering if total connected solar capacity on the local 11kV/415V distribution transformer exceeds 80% of its rated capacity. (*NEPRA S.R.O. 251(I)/2026 Reg 6*, 2026-02-09; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
3. **AEDB Installer Accreditation**: Dossiers must be submitted and signed by an AEDB/PPIB accredited solar installer (Category C-3 to C-A); unaccredited submissions are rejected at the portal stage. (*AEDB/PPIB Certification Rules*, 2024; [ppib.gov.pk](https://ppib.gov.pk)).
4. **PEC Engineer Stamp**: Single Line Diagrams (SLD) and technical design dossiers require endorsement and stamping by a licensed Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC) registered professional engineer. (*PEC Guidelines / LESCO Net Metering SOP*, 2024; [lesco.org.pk](https://lesco.org.pk)).
5. **Billing Name & CNIC Mismatch**: If the utility connection is in a previous owner's, landlord's, or deceased relative's name, DISCO requires formal ownership transfer before Schedule-I execution. (*LESCO Commercial Procedures*, 2023; [lesco.org.pk](https://lesco.org.pk)).
6. **Earth Pit Resistance Failure**: DISCO M&T inspection strictly verifies earth pit resistance; resistance above 5 Ohms (or lack of separate equipment and lightning earthing) causes automatic inspection failure. (*NEPRA DG Interconnection Standards / LESCO M&T Guidelines*, 2024; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
7. **Uncertified Inverters**: Inverters without international IEC 62109-1/2 (safety) and IEC 61727 / IEEE 1547 (anti-islanding) certificates are rejected during M&T lab verification. (*AEDB Technical Standards for Net Metering*, 2024; [ppib.gov.pk](https://ppib.gov.pk)).
8. **Outstanding Utility Arrears**: Active disputes, unpaid detection bills, or late payments on the reference number prevent Revenue Officer (RO) clearance. (*DISCO Citizen Charter*, 2024).
9. **Demand Note Payment Lapses**: Failing to pay the bi-directional meter Demand Note within its 15–30 day validity period results in cancellation of the connection requisition. (*DISCO Operational SOP*, 2024).
10. **Phase Unbalance & Single-Phase Submissions**: Applying for systems >5 kW on single-phase meters without prior three-phase conversion causes processing rejection. (*LESCO SOP*, 2024; [lesco.org.pk](https://lesco.org.pk)).
