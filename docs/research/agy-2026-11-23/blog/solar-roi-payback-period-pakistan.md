## 3. Key Sourced Facts (8–15)
1. **Base Residential Tariffs**: NEPRA notified base electricity tariffs for unprotected residential consumers ranging from Rs. 39.15/kWh (301–400 units) to Rs. 48.84/kWh (>700 units). (*NEPRA S.R.O. 1035(I)/2024*, 2024-07-12; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
2. **Effective Grid Unit Cost**: Factoring in 18% GST, Financing Cost Surcharge (Rs. 3.23/kWh), variable Fuel Charges Adjustment (FCA ~Rs. 2–4/kWh), and electricity duties, upper-slab domestic grid costs range between Rs. 55 to Rs. 68 per imported kWh. (*LESCO / IESCO Verified Billing Schedules*, 2024; [lesco.org.pk](https://lesco.org.pk)).
3. **Net Billing Buyback Rate**: Under NEPRA S.R.O. 251(I)/2026, exported solar power earns the wholesale National Average Energy Purchase Price (~Rs. 10.00–11.30/kWh), making behind-the-meter self-consumption roughly 4x to 5x more valuable than exporting. (*NEPRA S.R.O. 251(I)/2026*, 2026-02-09; [nepra.org.pk](https://nepra.org.pk)).
4. **Turnkey System Installation Benchmarks**: Standard Tier-1 on-grid residential solar systems in Pakistan cost approximately PKR 110 to PKR 135 per watt installed (e.g., PKR 1.15M–1.35M for a 10 kW system), driven by international silicon supply price drops. (*Pakistan Renewable Energy Coalition Market Bulletin*, 2024-10-15 [Secondary - Industry Report]).
5. **Equity Payback Benchmark**: For domestic consumers consuming >700 units/month, an on-grid 10 kW system saving 1,100–1,250 kWh/month achieves an equity payback period of approximately 2.2 to 3.2 years under 2024–2026 tariff structures. (*Renewable First / LUMS Energy Institute Solar Financial Model*, 2024).
6. **Annual PV Module Degradation**: High-quality N-type TOPCon panels exhibit ≤1.0% first-year degradation and ~0.40% annual linear degradation, retaining ≥88.8% nameplate generation at year 25. (*Jinko Tiger Neo / Longi Hi-MO X6 Datasheets*, 2024).
7. **Mid-Life Inverter Replacement Provision**: String inverters operate with an 8 to 12 year lifespan; an accurate 25-year financial model allocates an inverter replacement/overhaul reserve of ~15%–20% initial CAPEX around year 10. (*NREL Life Cycle Costing Guidelines for PV*, 2023; [nrel.gov](https://www.nrel.gov)).
8. **Annual O&M Expenditure**: Routine cleaning, biannual inspections, and minor electrical maintenance average 1.0% to 1.5% of initial CAPEX annually. (*PCRET Solar Economics Advisory*, 2023; [pcret.gov.pk](https://pcret.gov.pk)).
9. **Levelized Cost of Electricity (LCOE)**: Rooftop solar in Pakistan delivers electricity at an unsubsidized LCOE of approx. Rs. 7.50 to Rs. 9.50/kWh over 25 years, compared to retail grid rates of Rs. 45–65/kWh. (*Renewable First Levelized Cost Benchmark*, 2024).
10. **Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**: High-slab residential solar investments in Pakistan yield an internal rate of return of 28% to 36% under current tariff regimes, far outpacing bank deposits and mutual funds. (*LUMS Energy Institute Feasibility Model*, 2024).

- **What They Miss**: The impact of NEPRA's 2026 Net Billing wholesale buyback rate (~Rs. 11/kWh vs Rs. 40+ import), 0.4%/yr panel degradation, recurring cleaning OPEX, and the required capital reserve for mid-life inverter replacement at year 10.
