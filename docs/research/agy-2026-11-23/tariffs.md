# NEPRA Residential Electricity Tariff Summary (As of November 2026)

## 1. Regulatory Framework & DISCO Uniformity
Residential tariffs across **LESCO**, **IESCO**, and **FESCO** follow the **GoP Applicable Uniform Consumer-End Tariff** under Section 31(7) of the NEPRA Act, 1997. The latest tariff structure was determined in NEPRA's Decision dated **February 11, 2026** (No. TRF-100/2935-57) and gazetted by the Federal Government vide **S.R.O. 279(I)/2026** dated **February 12, 2026** (consequent to DISCO-specific baseline S.R.O.s 41(I)/2026 [FESCO], 45(I)/2026 [IESCO], and 46(I)/2026 [LESCO]).

## 2. Residential Base Slabs & Fixed Charges (Category A-1)
* **Protected Consumers** (<=200 units/mo consistently for 6 months; sanctioned load < 5 kW):
  * *Up to 50 Units (Lifeline)*: Rs. 3.95/kWh | Fixed: Rs. 0
  * *51–100 Units (Lifeline)*: Rs. 7.74/kWh | Fixed: Rs. 0
  * *001–100 Units*: Rs. 10.54/kWh | Fixed: Rs. 200/kW/month
  * *101–200 Units*: Rs. 13.01/kWh | Fixed: Rs. 300/kW/month
  *(Protected consumers retain the incremental benefit of one previous slab).*
* **Unprotected Consumers** (Sanctioned load < 5 kW):
  * *001–100 Units*: Rs. 22.44/kWh (Fixed: Rs. 275/kW/month)
  * *101–200 Units*: Rs. 28.91/kWh (Fixed: Rs. 300/kW/month)
  * *201–300 Units*: Rs. 33.10/kWh (Fixed: Rs. 350/kW/month)
  * *301–400 Units*: Rs. 36.46/kWh (Fixed: Rs. 400/kW/month)
  * *401–500 Units*: Rs. 38.95/kWh (Fixed: Rs. 500/kW/month)
  * *501–600 Units*: Rs. 40.22/kWh (Fixed: Rs. 675/kW/month)
  * *601–700 Units*: Rs. 41.85/kWh (Fixed: Rs. 675/kW/month)
  * *Above 700 Units*: Rs. 47.20/kWh (Fixed: Rs. 675/kW/month)
* **Time-of-Use (ToU)** (Sanctioned load >= 5 kW):
  * *Peak*: Rs. 46.85/kWh | *Off-Peak*: Rs. 34.53/kWh
  * *Fixed*: Rs. 675/kW/month applied to 50% of sanctioned load or recorded MDI, whichever is higher.
* **Minimum Charges**: Rs. 75/mo (single phase) / Rs. 150/mo (3-phase) only if no energy is used and fixed charges do not apply.

## 3. Bill Add-Ons & Statutory Levies
* **GST**: 18% standard rate under Sales Tax Act 1990 (levied on energy + fixed + FCA + QTA).
* **Electricity Duty**: 1.5% on cost of electricity (Punjab Finance Act 1964; ICT rules).
* **PTV Fee**: Rs. 35/month flat fee for residential connections.
* **Income Tax**: 7.5% withholding tax under Section 235 of Income Tax Ordinance 2001 for non-filers not on ATL with monthly bill >= Rs. 25,000 (0% for filers or bills < Rs. 25,000).
* **Fuel Charges Adjustment (FCA)**: Latest notified for September 2026 is **+Rs. 2.3673/kWh** (Decision dated 06-11-2026, TRF-100/MFPA/21785-804; billed in Nov 2026; lifeline exempt).
* **Quarterly Adjustment (QTA)**: Latest for 2nd Quarter CY 2026 is **+Rs. 0.5194/kWh** (S.R.O. 1501(I)/2026 dated 07-09-2026; billed Sep–Nov 2026; lifeline exempt).
* **Financing Cost Surcharge**: Rs. 3.23/kWh (notified under NEPRA Act Sec 31(5); lifeline exempt).

## 4. Open Doubts & Ambiguities
1. **Punjab Electricity Duty Exemption vs. Billing Reality**: Punjab legislative bills proposed exempting domestic users from the 1.5% duty; however, DISCO billing engines (LESCO/FESCO/IESCO) still compute and charge 1.5% on variable electricity cost.
2. **PTV Fee Abolition Status**: Federal executive announcements in mid-2025 declared the elimination of the Rs. 35 TV fee; however, formal statutory gazetting and uniform removal in DISCO ERPs remain inconsistent.
3. **Fixed Charges Billing on Analog Meters**: Under S.R.O. 279(I)/2026, non-ToU fixed charges are billed per kW sanctioned load. On legacy meters without MDI, billing strictly relies on paper sanctioned load rather than actual load.
4. **FCA Applicability Window**: FCA is determined with a two-month lag (Sept 2026 FCA notified Nov 2026). October 2026 FCA was under hearing in late November 2026.
