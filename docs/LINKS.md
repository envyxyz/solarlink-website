# Links and mentions: 90-day plan

Goal: a few real local and industry links and mentions, not a large number. Expect a few real links a month at best; that is enough. Watch map position and enquiries, not link counts.

The owner sends or approves each message in this plan. Nothing goes out in SolarLink's name without that.

## Days 1 to 30: foundations

1. The owner approves the NAP blocks in `docs/CITATIONS.md`. Nothing else starts before this.
2. Set up the profiles in the order in CITATIONS.md: Google (per staffed outlet, see `docs/GBP.md`), Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Facebook page. Each gets the website URL of its own outlet page.
3. Fix wrong listings first. Google each old phone number and address variant (Munawar and Munawer, 54000 and 54700, the Lahore number shown for Islamabad or Sargodha). List every duplicate in the CITATIONS.md log, then merge or remove it. The owner sends or approves each removal request.
4. Add the free directories from CITATIONS.md that answered the check.
5. Claim what is owed. List suppliers, manufacturers or associations that SolarLink genuinely buys from or belongs to [client to confirm which], and look for their "where to buy" or installer pages. The owner sends or approves each request.
6. Find unlinked mentions: search `"SolarLink" -solarlink.com.pk` in Google, plus Facebook and YouTube. Where a page names SolarLink without a link, the owner sends or approves a short thank-you asking for one.
7. Start the review routine in GBP.md.

## Days 31 to 60: assets worth linking to

Build only what is on the page plan (`src/routes.ts`) and only with verified figures. Each tool shows its source and date. A page marked with unmet needs in routes.ts is not pitched until those are met.

Steps:
1. For each asset, find 10 to 20 real places a reader would already look: the society's forum, a local news section, a campus society page, a farmers' group. The owner approves the list.
2. Use the outreach template: short, personal, about their readers, from the owner's real address. Follow up once after 4 to 7 days. The owner sends or approves each message.
3. Real partner links: non-competing businesses SolarLink already works with, only where the relationship is real.
4. Answer real questions in community threads as SolarLink, disclosed, with a link only where it answers the question. The owner approves each post.

## Days 61 to 90: mentions for AI answers

AI answers recommend companies that independent pages mention. Run 10 to 15 customer-style prompts ("solar company in Lahore", "best solar installer Islamabad", "SolarLink phone number") in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google, 3 times each, and record who is named and which pages are cited. Those pages are the targets.

## What never to do

- Buy links, guest posts or "DA" packages.
- Run link exchanges at scale.
- Write or buy reviews, or review-gate.
- Redirect expired domains into the site.
- Post the same message across many groups.
- Use any claim from `docs/BANNED-CLAIMS.md` in a pitch.
